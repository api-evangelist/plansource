Lake Forest, Ill. – October 13, 2026 – Trustmark Voluntary Benefits® has joined the Boost carrier integration program, bringing its voluntary benefits portfolio into PlanSource’s connected benefits platform. Access to industry-leading life insurance with long-term care, critical illness, hospital, accident, and disability coverage is now available for mutual customers of Trustmark and PlanSource.
